# Governance summary

M&Ms must not be described as a fully preregistered study: development and
prior observations preceded its confirmatory endpoint freeze. The registered
endpoint procedures were frozen before the corresponding confirmatory
readout. AbdomenCT Validation-2 had a more prospectively complete preflight
freeze, including eligibility, splits, estimands and decision rules; recorded
technical amendments belong to that lineage and do not authorize changing
endpoints after readout. No post-readout endpoint selection was introduced.
Claim finalization is distinct from prospective protocol registration.

The reported output lineage belongs to frozen R5/R8 endpoint records and the
manuscript figure/result ledger. Those numeric artefacts are outside the initial software scope; source-data-dependent reproduction regenerates inputs locally. No reported estimates are bundled. Supporting exploratory analysis is distinct
from confirmatory endpoints.

Segmentation training labels, QC_TRAIN quality labels, CALIBRATION residuals,
evaluation labels and locked final-test labels have separate access roles.
Splits are patient-level. True target quality is never a deployment feature.
Source support statistics remain source-fitted. Access to final target masks
requires SHIFTQC_UNLOCK_FINAL=1 through the guarded evaluation path; no such
access is made in this preparation task.

This public-facing summary excludes internal discussions and chat.
AI-assisted tools supported software/documentation/audit preparation.
Scientific responsibility remains with the human author; AI is not an author.
